Our Story

Crafting Beauty
Since 2016

Nestled in the heart of IBD Emporia Mall, Kolar Road, Krishna Unisex Family Salon was born from a singular passion — to make every individual feel extraordinary. What began as a dream has blossomed into Bhopal's most trusted premium salon destination.

We believe beauty is not a destination but a journey — one that deserves the finest care, the most skilled hands, and an atmosphere that soothes the soul. From precision haircuts to transformative skin treatments, every service is a ritual crafted just for you.

Our team of certified professionals stay ahead of global trends, combining international techniques with personalized care to deliver results that exceed expectations. This is not just a salon — it is your personal sanctuary.
